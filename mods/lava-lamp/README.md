# Lava Lamp

Capri as a 70s lava lamp: violet glass with glowing wax blobs rising, merging and pinching apart inside her, a warm glow from below, glowing wax panels and a brushed chrome base.

- Kind: skin
- Made by: Vocyn

Her shapes and animations are her own, only restyled, and everything in it loops with her. Turn it off on the Mods page to get her normal look back.
