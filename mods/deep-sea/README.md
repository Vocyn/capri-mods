# Deep Sea

Capri in the abyss: ocean-blue hair and body under rippling caustic light, small glowing jellyfish drifting up through her hair, pulsing photophores along her seams, rising bubbles and glowing sea-glass panels.

- Kind: skin
- Made by: Vocyn

Her shapes and animations are her own, only restyled, and everything in it loops with her. Turn it off on the Mods page to get her normal look back.
