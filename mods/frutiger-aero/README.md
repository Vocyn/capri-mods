# Frutiger Aero

Capri in the glossy mid-2000s look of Windows Vista and 7, all inside her own outline: water-glass blue hair full of rising bubbles, orange gel panels, gel-gloss shards, black-glass limbs and twinkling lens flares.

- Kind: skin
- Made by: Vocyn

Her shapes and animations are exactly her own, only restyled, and nothing is drawn outside her, so she's her usual size. Colour mods still work on top of it. Turn it off on the Mods page to get her normal look back.

Built from Capri's art, so it's under the same PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0: free to share, not to sell. Capri's art and animations are by her artist.

New in 3.3.0: she stands in her new 0.8.6 loop in this skin, facing either way, with its blink, and keeps its old stand for Legacy stand in Settings. Needs Capri 0.8.6 or later.
