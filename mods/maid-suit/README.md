# Maid Suit

A skin for Capri: a maid suit, for you weirdos.

- Kind: skin
- Made by: Vocyn

Turn it on in Capri's Mods window. A pleated dress, ruffled apron, puffed sleeves, cuffs, bows and a frilled headpiece, in every pose but her dance.

New in 2.0.4: the shards of hair beside her face no longer flash black. They stay red in every frame of her sitting and lying clips, including blinks, glitches, music, arriving and leaving.
