# Hair Down

Capri with her hair let down: one sleek hot-pink sheet with a neon glow, falling down her back to a hooked point, with a thin strand in front of her face.

- Kind: skin
- Made by: Vocyn

It sways a little as she moves, drapes down her back when she sits, spills off the end of her ledge when she lies down, and glitches and beams in and out with her. Everything else is her own art and animation. Colour mods recolour the hair along with the rest of her.
