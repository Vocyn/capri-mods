# Custom Names

Capri answers to more names: Nova, Big Z and Capricorn. She still answers to Capri too.

- Kind: settings mod
- Made by: Vocyn

Say "hey Nova, what time is it?" or "ask Capricorn to open notepad" and she hears you the same as "Capri". To pick your own names, copy this file, change `wake_names` and `id`, and import it in Capri's Mods tab.
