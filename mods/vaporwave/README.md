# Vaporwave

Capri in 2010s vaporwave: sunset hair with a striped retro sun and a swaying palm, a neon grid rolling down her legs, pastel marble, aqua tiles, VHS scanlines, tracking wobble and colour bleed.

- Kind: skin
- Made by: Vocyn

Her shapes and animations are her own, only restyled, and everything in it loops with her. Turn it off on the Mods page to get her normal look back.
