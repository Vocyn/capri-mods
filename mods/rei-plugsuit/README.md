# Rei Plugsuit

Capri in a white plugsuit inspired by Rei Ayanami: pale blue hair, two white interface clips on her head, a red chest core, shoulder pylons with orange tops, navy limbs, green plates, black leg bands and red eyes.

- Kind: skin
- Made by: Vocyn

Her shapes and animations are her own, only restyled, and everything in it loops with her. Turn it off on the Mods page to get her normal look back. The clips and the chest core follow her in every pose, and glitch and beam with her. An unofficial fan homage to Neon Genesis Evangelion, not affiliated with its creators.

Built from Capri's art, so it's under the same PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0: free to share, not to sell. Capri's art and animations are by her artist.

New in 2.1.0: she stands in her new 0.8.6 loop in this skin, facing either way, with its blink, and keeps its old stand for Legacy stand in Settings. Needs Capri 0.8.6 or later.
