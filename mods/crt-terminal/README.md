# CRT Terminal

Capri on an old green-phosphor monitor: scanlines and glow, code rain falling through her hair, a refresh band rolling down her and a block cursor blinking on her chest. Her glitches turn amber, like the tube losing sync.

- Kind: skin
- Made by: Vocyn

Her shapes and animations are her own, only restyled, and everything in it loops with her. Turn it off on the Mods page to get her normal look back.

Built from Capri's art, so it's under the same PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0: free to share, not to sell. Capri's art and animations are by her artist.

New in 1.1.0: she stands in her new 0.8.6 loop in this skin, facing either way, with its blink, and keeps its old stand for Legacy stand in Settings. Needs Capri 0.8.6 or later.
