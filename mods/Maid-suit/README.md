# Maid Suit

A maid outfit for Capri: pleated dress, ruffled apron, puffed sleeves, cuffs, bows and a frilled headpiece. Her hair and face stay the same.

- Kind: skin
- Made by: Vocyn

New in 2.0.4: the shards of hair beside her face no longer flash black. They stay red in every frame of her sitting and lying clips, including blinks, glitches, music, arriving and leaving.

Turn it off on the Mods page to get her normal look back. Colour mods still work on top of it.
